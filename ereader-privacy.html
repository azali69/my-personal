My Personal E-Reader

A personal PDF reader for Android (phone and BOOX e-ink tablets) with highlights, handwriting, notes, offline OCR and an offline dictionary. Privacy policy · last updated 25 October 2026.

What the app stores

Your books, notes, highlights, handwriting, bookmarks and reading position are stored only on your own device. The app has no server of its own, no advertising and no analytics or tracking.

Google sign-in and Google Drive

Sync between your devices is optional. If you choose “Sign in with Google”, the app asks for one permission: access to its own hidden application-data folder in your Google Drive (drive.appdata). The app cannot see any other files in your Drive. It also reads your Google account email address only to show which account is syncing.

The app saves one file there (MyPersonalEReader-sync.json) containing your notes, highlights, handwriting, bookmarks, layers and reading positions, so your other devices can read it. Your PDF files are not uploaded. This data goes directly between your device and Google; it is not sent to the developer or anyone else.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing your data

Choose “Sign out (stop syncing here)” in the app. To delete the synced file from Google Drive, open Google Drive on the web → Settings → Manage apps → My Personal E-Reader → Delete hidden app data. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on that device.

Other features

Text recognition (OCR) and the dictionary run fully offline on the device. Downloading a book from a link you enter connects only to that link.
